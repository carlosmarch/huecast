A new kind of weather app. Not a dashboard full of numbers, a sky. Huecast reads the temperature, the hour, the cloud, rain, snow, fog and wind for any place on Earth, and paints them.

Colour from
the real sky

The background blends a time-of-day colour with a temperature colour. Sunrise always gets the dawn colours and sunset the evening ones, so a short winter day keeps its light in step with the sun.

Weather,
drawn live

Each kind of weather has steps, set by the measured amount: heavier rain falls faster, heavier snow has bigger flakes, thicker fog rises higher, and wind slants the rain. Only low, thick cloud hides the sun.

24 hours
you can scrub

The bars are the next day. Each one is a tiny copy of that hour's sky, its height is the temperature, and ticks above it mark the rain. Drag across them and the whole screen moves through the day. Hold them for the week ahead.
