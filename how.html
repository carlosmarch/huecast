How to read
the sky

Everything on screen is drawn from the forecast for that place and hour. No photos, no icons. New here? Start with the overview ›

01

Sky & circle

The background mixes a colour for the time of day (following the real sunrise and sunset) with one for the temperature. Clouds, fog and rain drain the colour; snow brightens it.

Temperature

The circle is the sun by day and a ring (the moon) at night. The hotter it is, the more it pulses. Thick cloud veils it, and below −5° an ice halo appears.

04

Buttons

  • Search any city; pinned places come first
  • Random somewhere extreme right now; hold to choose which
  • My location the weather where you are
  • Pin keep the place at the top of search

05

Tips

  • Hold the sky for wallpaper view: only the sky and the sun or moon. Tap it to hide the buttons.
  • Add it to your home screen from the share menu. It works offline with the last forecast.

06

Privacy

Forecasts come from Open‑Meteo, city names from BigDataCloud and Open‑Meteo. Your location only goes to them. No server, no account, no tracking: pins, units and your last place stay in this browser.
